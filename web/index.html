Case

Case options are the variants and severities declared by this procedure's authored traces. Perform microsteps from the list in the main view — in any order: the engine resolves what was actually done, including clipping or dividing before the safety gate is demonstrated. The virtual clock starts at 0 s and advances 60 s per action, the median inter-action gap of the authored clean traces.

The workbench board beside the inputs is computed by the case engine from the declared priors and likelihood ratios. Adjudication is revealed only after the final stage.
