**Evidence on VR/haptics transfer (sober summary):** (1) VR training *to proficiency* transfers to the OR (Seymour; Sroka — Fronts 2/11). (2) **Haptics specifically: favourable but not decisive.** A 2024 review of 51 studies found more results favouring haptic feedback than not, with high heterogeneity. A 2025 systematic review/meta-analysis of **7 RCTs (n=125)** comparing haptic VR vs physical box trainers found **box trainers reached proficiency faster, and no significant difference in skill transfer** — i.e., cheap physical trainers remain embarrassingly competitive, and VR's edge is logistics (anywhere, instrumented, repeatable), not magic touch. (3) Visual-dominant / low-haptic systems repeatedly improve *in-simulator* metrics with weak far transfer to force-sensitive tasks. **Design rule: budget haptics for force-critical steps only (needle driving, dissection planes, drilling breakthrough), run the haptic servo at ~1 kHz in its own thread/process, and never let headset frame rate gate force rendering.**
