**What a from-scratch educational engine should borrow (architecture, not hype):** (1) 0-D **compartment / circuit analogs** — nodes with compliance/capacitance, paths with resistance/inertance — for cardiovascular and respiratory systems; (2) substance transport between compartments as the single mechanism for gases, drugs (PK), metabolites; (3) homeostatic feedback as explicit controllers on top, so learners can break them; (4) a **common data model + per-system validation table against published means/ranges/waveforms**, Pulse-style; (5) fixed small timestep with faster-than-real-time headroom (Pulse runs 10–15× real-time on a laptop). **Open normal/pathological reference values** exist in Pulse's methodology reports, the Physiome/CellML repository, and Guyton-lineage models — cite the value's source in the case file, never hard-code unsourced numbers (Front 11 rule).
