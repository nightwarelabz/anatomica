### 5. Clinical Reasoning / Diagnosis Training

**How leading tools teach it (all closed as products; the pedagogy is the takeaway):** **DXplain** (MGH — ranked differential from findings, with “why” explanations and rare-disease prompting), **Isabel** (symptom-checker-derived differential lists used for diagnostic-error reduction), **Human Diagnosis Project / Human Dx** (crowd + AI collective diagnosis on case vignettes, “Human Dx” measured human+machine accuracy), **iatroX and similar** (case-based Bayesian updating tutors). Common proven mechanics: forced generation of a differential *before* seeing suggestions (fights anchoring/premature closure), explicit pre-test probability → finding-by-finding updating, and feedback on **calibration** (confidence vs accuracy), which the literature shows is trainable but decays without spaced practice. *(Product pages for DXplain/Isabel/Human Dx were not pinned in this pass — treat descriptions as background, verify before citing in-product.)*
