### 7. Infectious Disease & Antimicrobials

**IDSA Practice Guidelines — Infectious Diseases Society of America (in *Clinical Infectious Diseases*, Oxford University Press).**
Content: GRADE-based guidelines + non-graded guidance documents (A–Z list: CAP, SSTI, MRSA/AMR guidance, candidiasis, Lyme, cUTI updates, etc.). Access reality is *mixed*: IDSA guideline landing pages and many full texts are freely readable on idsociety.org/OUP (IDSA makes guidelines available without charge in practice, incl. the 2026 AMR gram-negative guidance reported as free open access), but the copyright line remains **© IDSA/OUP, all rights reserved, no reproduction/text-mining without permission**. “Free to read ≠ free to reuse.”
Openness: **CITE-ONLY.** Link to the guideline page per-case; encode recommendations as original decision logic with citation; never mirror PDFs or copy tables. URL: https://www.idsociety.org/practice-guideline/all-practice-guidelines/ — *index*.

**Sanford Guide to Antimicrobial Therapy — Antimicrobial Therapy, Inc. (since 1969; continuously/daily updated digital).**
Openness: **CLOSED.** Subscription app/web/print (~$50+/user personal; institutional licences via Stanford-style library deals). No open tier for content. Do not scrape, do not reproduce its tables, do not imply endorsement. The product may teach *that* clinicians check a current local guide/antibiogram — behaviour, not content. URL (access model evidence): https://laneblog.stanford.edu/2025/07/01/be-an-antimicrobial-stewardship-with-sanford-guide/ — *index*.

**Open antimicrobial layer that CAN be built:** CDC resources (below, public-domain); university stewardship guidebooks that are posted openly (e.g. Stanford Antimicrobial Safety & Sustainability Program pages — note current guidebook access is SUNet-gated via Sanford app: treat as **institution-gated, cite-only** unless a page is explicitly open); Johns Hopkins ABX Guide is likewise subscription. Practical build rule: spectrum tables authored *de novo* from drug labelling + CDC/IDSA-cited facts, reviewed by a clinician, versioned — never derived by copying Sanford/Hopkins tables.

**CDC — guidelines, outbreak data, stewardship (US federal).**
Numbers/principles: **Core Elements of Hospital Antibiotic Stewardship (2014, updated 2019) — 7 elements:** (1) leadership commitment, (2) accountability, (3) pharmacy expertise, (4) action (incl. **48-hour “antibiotic timeout,”** prospective audit-and-feedback, pre-authorisation), (5) tracking, (6) reporting, (7) education. Stewardship teaching spine for cases: right drug/dose/route/duration, narrowest effective spectrum, IV→PO switch, de-escalate on cultures, don't treat colonisation/asymptomatic bacteriuria.
Openness: **BUILD — CDC works are US-government/public-domain in practice** (attribute CDC; check per-page notices for third-party content). URLs: https://www.in.gov/health/idepd/healthcare-associated-infections-and-antimicrobial-resistance-epidemiology/antimicrobial-stewardship/acute-care (verbatim CDC list); https://www.cdc.gov/nhsn/pdfs/training/2019/antibiotic-stewardship-508.pdf — *index*.

**Sepsis pathogen epidemiology — published distributions (teach as ranges, geography-dependent).**
Content: No universal distribution exists — the honest engine prior is *source-first*: pneumonia is the commonest source in most ICU cohorts (~30% in the cited severe-sepsis cohort: pneumonia 29.7%, intra-abdominal 18.8%, urinary 17.2%, skin/soft-tissue 10.9%), with urinary-first ordering in some ward/blood-culture cohorts (UTI 17.0% as first source in a 2,789-patient bacteraemic series). Organisms: *E. coli* consistently #1 (~21–32% across cited cohorts), then *Klebsiella pneumoniae* (~14–22%), *S. aureus* (~11%); Gram-negative predominance is marked in Asian cohorts (70.3%) vs a more balanced Gram+/Gram− split in Western ICUs; culture-negativity up to ~30% does not exclude sepsis. MDR rates are intensely local (26.6% in the cited cohort) — the workbench must parameterise by setting/antibiogram, not ship one global prior as truth.
Openness: **BUILD as cited ranges** (open-access cohort papers). URL: https://www.mdpi.com/2075-1729/16/1/121 — *index*.
