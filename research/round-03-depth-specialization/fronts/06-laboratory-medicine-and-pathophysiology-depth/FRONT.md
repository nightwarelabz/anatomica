### 6. Laboratory Medicine & Pathophysiology Depth

**Cell-based model of coagulation — Hoffman & Monroe, *Thromb Haemost* 2001.**
Content: Replaces the 1964 Davie–Ratnoff/Macfarlane cascade *as the in-vivo model* (cascade retained as the correct model of PT/aPTT assays). Three overlapping phases on cell surfaces: **(1) Initiation** on the tissue-factor-bearing cell (TF–VIIa → IXa/Xa → picomolar thrombin); **(2) Amplification** (thrombin activates platelets + V/VIII/XI, VIII released from vWF); **(3) Propagation** on the activated platelet surface (IXa–VIIIa tenase + Xa–Va prothrombinase → thrombin burst → fibrin). This is the model that explains why haemophilia bleeds despite an intact “extrinsic” pathway, and why PT/aPTT can mislead about in-vivo bleeding — the causal-chain structure the reasoning engine should implement, with cascade view kept as the “lab-test view.”

URL: https://derangedphysiology.com/main/cicm-primary-exam/haematological-system/Chapter-018/haemostasis-and-coagulation (open teaching synthesis citing Hoffman) — *index*.

**Reference-range methodology — CLSI EP28-A3c, *Defining, Establishing, and Verifying Reference Intervals* (2010, reaffirmed 2020).**
Numbers: Reference interval = **central 95%** of a carefully defined healthy reference population, limits at the **2.5th and 97.5th percentiles** — by definition **5% of healthy people fall outside** (2.5% each tail); “abnormal flag ≠ disease” must be a first-class engine concept. De-novo establishment: **≥120 reference individuals per partition** (sex/age partitions multiply: a full chemistry profile can need ~1,000); non-parametric limits at n=120 are the 3rd and 118th ranked values, allowing 90% CIs on the limits. *Verification* (adopting a manufacturer's interval): only **20** reference individuals per partition. Parametric alternative (Gaussian): mean ±1.96 SD, ≥40 observations in teaching summaries.

**Critical values (panic values) — Lundberg concept (1972); CLIA/CAP/Joint Commission reporting duty.**
Numbers (representative adult lists — *every lab sets its own; the engine must present these as institution-variable*): K⁺ **<2.5–2.8 / >6.0–6.5 mEq/L**; Na⁺ **<120 / >160 mmol/L**; glucose **<40 / >450–500 mg/dL**; total calcium **<6.0 / >13.0 mg/dL**; ionised calcium <0.78 / >1.58 mmol/L. Reporting expectation: immediate notification (typically within 30–60 min of availability) with read-back/documentation. Mechanistic chains (“why labs move”) should be authored per-analyte from physiology (e.g. K⁺ in AKI/DKA shifts, Ca²⁺ in malignancy via PTHrP) — no single open source covers this; Deranged Physiology / open physiology texts are the practical BUILD source family, each chain individually cited in-product.
