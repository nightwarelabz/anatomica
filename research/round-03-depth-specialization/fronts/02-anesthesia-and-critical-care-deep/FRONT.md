### 2. Anesthesia & Critical Care, Deep

**2022 ASA Practice Guidelines for Management of the Difficult Airway — American Society of Anesthesiologists (*Anesthesiology*, Jan 2022; 51 pp.; revision of 2013).**
Content/numbers: New pre-induction *awake-vs-asleep decision tool* — consider awake intubation if difficult laryngoscopy/intubation anticipated **plus any one of**: (1) difficult ventilation (mask/SGA), (2) ↑ aspiration risk, (3) intolerance of brief apnoea / rapid desaturation risk, (4) difficult emergency invasive access. *OPTIMIZE OXYGENATION THROUGHOUT* (low/high-flow nasal O₂ from before airway management through extubation; head-elevated position). Confirm ventilation with **exhaled CO₂ (capnography)** — pulse oximetry lags. *LIMIT ATTEMPTS*: reasonable (not mandatory) limit **3 attempts + 1 by a more skilled clinician** per modality (mask, SGA, intubation); be aware of passage of time; call for help / invasive access in the emergency pathway; consider awakening the patient in the non-emergency cannot-intubate pathway. First-ever **paediatric** difficult-airway infographic + extubation strategy section; video laryngoscopy to be readily available.
Openness: **CITE-ONLY.** ASA guideline + infographics are ASA-copyright (journal paywall ecosystem). Encode the decision logic in original words/diagrams; never ship the ASA infographic images. Summary source (APSF, free to read): https://www.apsf.org/article/anesthesia-patient-safety-foundation-update-2022-american-society-of-anesthesiologists-practice-guidelines-for-management-of-the-difficult-airway/ — *index*.

**ARDSNet ARMA / NIH Ventilator Protocol — NHLBI ARDS Network (*NEJM* 2000; n=861).**
Numbers: Tidal volume dosed to **predicted body weight** (PBW: male 50 + 2.3×[height in − 60]; female 45.5 + 2.3×[height in − 60]). Start **VT 6 mL/kg PBW (protocol range 4–8)** vs traditional 12 mL/kg. **Plateau pressure ≤30 cmH₂O** — if Pplat >30, ↓VT by 1 mL/kg steps to floor 4; if Pplat <25 and VT <6, ↑ by 1 mL/kg back toward 6; VT may rise toward 8 only for severe dyspnoea/breath-stacking or pH <7.15 with Pplat ≤30. Oxygenation goal **PaO₂ 55–80 mmHg / SpO₂ 88–95%** via FiO₂/PEEP table (PEEP 5–24); pH goal 7.30–7.45, RR to max 35. Result: mortality **31.0% vs 39.8%**, more ventilator-free days; stopped early.
Openness: **BUILD the numbers** (US-government network protocol; facts) with citation; do not copy protocol-card artwork verbatim. URL: https://litfl.com/ardsnet-ventilation-strategy/ (LITFL summary of the primary protocol) — *index*.

**Transfusion thresholds — TRICC (Hébert et al., *NEJM* 1999; n=838) → 2023 AABB International Guidelines (*JAMA* 2023; 45 RCTs, 20,599 adults).**
Numbers: TRICC restrictive (**transfuse <7 g/dL, target 7–9**) vs liberal (<10): 30-day mortality 18.7% vs 23.3% (NS overall; in-hospital mortality lower restrictive). AABB 2023 (strong, moderate-certainty): **<7 g/dL** for haemodynamically stable hospitalised adults; **7.5 g/dL** cardiac surgery; **8 g/dL** orthopaedic surgery or pre-existing cardiovascular disease; **<7 g/dL** also for stable critically ill children (with congenital-heart strata 7 / 9 / 7–9 g/dL by repair stage). Caution flag for the case library: MINT (2023, MI + anaemia, n≈3,500) trended *against* restrictive (death/recurrent MI ~17% vs ~15%) — teach acute-MI anaemia as contested, not settled at 7.
Openness: **CITE-ONLY for AABB text** (JAMA/OUP-style paywall; AABB Standards are paid products). Threshold *numbers* are encodable facts. URL (guideline abstract text): https://www.researchgate.net/publication/374681890_Red_Blood_Cell_Transfusion_2023_AABB_International_Guidelines — *index*.

**ICU Liberation Bundle (A–F) — SCCM; grounded in PADIS Guidelines (2018; focused update 2025).**
Content: **A** Assess/prevent/manage pain; **B** Both spontaneous awakening trials + spontaneous breathing trials; **C** Choice of analgesia/sedation (analgesia-first, light sedation, avoid benzodiazepines where possible); **D** Delirium assess/prevent/manage (CAM-ICU/ICDSC); **E** Early mobility/exercise; **F** Family engagement/empowerment. SCCM-cited collaborative evidence (>20,000 patients in secondary summaries): dose-response — higher bundle compliance → lower death, delirium/coma days, restraint use, ICU readmission (teach as association from implementation collaboratives, not an RCT effect size).
Openness: **BUILD the bundle structure as original teaching logic; CITE-ONLY for PADIS/SCCM text.** SCCM bundle materials are freely readable on sccm.org but remain SCCM-copyright. URL: https://sccm.org/blog/exploring-sccm-the-icu-centers-of-excellence-program — *index*.

**ASRA Antithrombotic Guidelines, 5th edition — American Society of Regional Anesthesia and Pain Medicine (*Reg Anesth Pain Med*, 2025) + ASRA LAST Checklist (2020).**
Numbers (needle placement / neuraxial or *deep plexus/peripheral* blocks — same intervals): rename “prophylactic/therapeutic” → **low dose / high dose**. Enoxaparin low dose **≥12 h**, high dose **≥24 h**; SC heparin 5,000 U 2–3×/day **4–6 h** (or normal coags); apixaban high dose **≥72 h**, low dose (2.5 mg BID) **≥36 h**; rivaroxaban high dose **≥72 h**, low dose (10 mg) **≥24 h** (30 h if CrCl <30); dabigatran high dose **≥72 h** (CrCl ≥50) / **≥120 h** (CrCl 30–49); clopidogrel **5–7 days**; warfarin **5 days + normalised INR**. Acceptable residual: DOAC level **<30 ng/mL** or anti-Xa **≤0.1 IU/mL**. Superficial/compressible peripheral blocks are *not* held to neuraxial intervals (ASRA scope limit; Tsui/CAS advisory stratifies the rest). LAST: stop injection, call for help, **20% lipid emulsion early**, modified ACLS (epinephrine ≤1 mcg/kg, avoid vasopressin/CCB/beta-blocker/lidocaine).

Encode intervals as a queryable table in original form with “verify against current ASRA guideline” labelling. URLs: https://lms.ehc.gov.eg/lms/mod/book/tool/print/index.php?id=742 (executive summary mirror); https://www.forgeanesthesia.com/nce-regional-anesthesia.html (LAST + intervals summary) — *index*.

**RASS — Richmond Agitation–Sedation Scale (Sessler et al., 2002, Virginia Commonwealth University).**
Numbers: 10 points, **−5 to +4**: +4 combative, +3 very agitated, +2 agitated, +1 restless, **0 alert & calm**, −1 drowsy (eye contact >10 s to voice), −2 light sedation (<10 s), −3 moderate (movement/eye opening, no eye contact), −4 deep (responds only to physical stimulus), −5 unarousable. Usual ICU target in PADIS-era practice: light sedation **−2 to 0/+1** unless deep sedation specifically indicated. Assessment order: observe → voice → physical stimulus.
Openness: **BUILD — the scale is freely reproduced in practice** (Physiopedia lists it as freely available online); still credit Sessler/VCU and reproduce wording minimally/paraphrased where possible. URL: https://www.physio-pedia.com/Richmond_Agitation-Sedation_Scale_(RASS) — *index*.

**Vasopressors — SOAP II (De Backer et al., *NEJM* 2010; n=1,679) + Surviving Sepsis Campaign 2021 (SCCM/ESICM).**
Numbers: SOAP II dopamine vs norepinephrine: 28-day death 52.5% vs 48.5% (OR 1.17, p=0.10, NS) but arrhythmic events **24.1% vs 12.4% (p<0.001)**; cardiogenic-shock subgroup mortality higher with dopamine. SSC 2021 order: **(1) norepinephrine** first-line, **MAP target ≥65 mmHg**; **(2) add vasopressin 0.03 units/min** (fixed; rather than escalating NE beyond ~0.25–0.5 mcg/kg/min); **(3) add epinephrine**; dobutamine added for cardiac dysfunction/hypoperfusion despite MAP; hydrocortisone 200 mg/day in ongoing refractory shock. Hour-1 bundle context: lactate, cultures before antibiotics, broad-spectrum antibiotics, **30 mL/kg crystalloid** for hypotension/lactate ≥4, vasopressors for MAP <65 (teach 30 mL/kg as the 2021 “suggested” starting volume — contested since, see CLOVERS/CLASSIC-era debate; do not hard-code as unquestionable).
Openness: **CITE-ONLY** (NEJM / *Crit Care Med* texts). Numbers encodable. URLs: https://www.uspharmacist.com/article/vasopressor-use-in-the-icu ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov:443/articles/PMC13464546/ — *index*.
