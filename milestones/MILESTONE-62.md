The therapy microsteps are required in **no** configuration, deliberately: whether therapy is owed, and which modalities, is a function of the lesion's true Forrest class — a clean-based ulcer must receive none — so therapy is judged by the scoring rules against the scenario truth (§4), not by the checklist. The checklist therefore measures the diagnostic and process spine (27), exactly as the appendicectomy's closure microsteps are configuration-inserted rather than file-required.

**Process ceiling 4 of 5 by design:** no antibiotic prophylaxis act exists in this procedure's consulted management pathway (T49; T50), so no microstep carries the prophylaxis marker — the thyroid pattern (Milestone 56), documented in the procedure's scoring block.

## 3. The gate — identification and Forrest classification before therapy

`gate.identify-classify-before-therapy` (source T49), three criteria:

- **crit1 — bleeding source identified and localised** (m3.1). In the Dieulafoy configuration the account is only complete when the caliber-persistent artery search (m3.3) has been made.
- **crit2 — lesion classified by Forrest stigmata before therapy** (m3.2, declared at dp3). The classification is to be used in all patients with peptic ulcer hemorrhage (ESGE 2021), because it alone separates the lesions that receive therapy from those that must not, and the modalities each class requires.
- **crit3 — airway and sedation account made** (m1.2): aspiration risk assessed, plan chosen against it, sedation delivered with the ASGE monitoring set (pulse oximetry, ECG, intermittent BP, capnography considered for deep sedation) by personnel able to rescue an unresponsive patient (T53). Not therapy-blocking — its absence is priced by the safety score and, in the hematemesis configuration, by the unprotected-airway rule (§5).

**Enforcement is the house master rule on this procedure's gated verb.** Therapy is a new verb, `treat`, dispatched only for this procedure. A treat act taken with crit1 or crit2 undemonstrated, or with no classification declared at dp3, resolves as **err.treat-before-classification** (critical-fail): a perforation is computed onto the treated segment (canal.duodenum for a duodenal lesion, dig.stomach for a gastric/Dieulafoy lesion — T52: diagnostic perforation ≤0.03%, the risk rising with therapeutic intervention) and the attempt **FAILS at the act**, regardless of all other scores. The safety score reads, as always, criteria demonstrated before the first gated act plus the pre-therapy pause (m4.1 carries the existing `pre-division-pause` marker; no engine change was needed for the pause).

## 4. Judged against the truth — the Forrest scoring rules

The scenario carries the lesion truth in a new case field, `lesion` (`"duodenal-ulcer:iia"`, `"gastric-ulcer:ib"`, `"dieulafoy:gastric"`), and the attempt's dp3 declaration is recorded separately. At scoring (failed attempts excluded — their outcome is the perforation), the delivered modalities are judged **against the truth, not the declaration**, on the guidelines' own partition (high-risk Ia/Ib/IIa · adherent clot IIb · low-risk IIc/III):

- Declared group ≠ truth group → **err.misclassification** (major). The delivered therapy is then judged as the truth required.
- Truth high-risk, no therapy → **err.high-risk-untreated** (major) + **rebleeding computed** (structural; untreated further-bleeding rates 55% Ia/Ib, 43% IIa — T51).
- Truth high-risk, epinephrine only → **err.epinephrine-monotherapy** (major) + rebleeding. The one configuration both guidelines prohibit by name (ESGE strong/high; ACG statement 10, strong).
- Truth Dieulafoy, no therapy → err.high-risk-untreated + rebleeding; therapy without a mechanical modality → **err.substandard-modality** (major) + rebleeding (T54: banding/clipping most effective, ~90% permanent hemostasis; injection suboptimal, early recurrence in two of three injection/APC-only cases in the consulted series).
- Truth IIb, therapy delivered without clot removal first → **err.clot-not-addressed** (major). Recorded divergence: ACG makes no recommendation for or against therapy of an irrigation-resistant clot, where ESGE suggests clot removal and treatment of the exposed stigmata — the procedure follows ESGE and carries the divergence in its authority basis rather than resolving it by assertion. (Unexercised by any shipped trace; the rule is guarded, symmetric with its neighbours, and pinned by a unit test, §9.)
- Truth low-risk, any therapy → **err.therapy-not-indicated** (major, no complication computed — the departure is the exposure itself).
- Ia/Ib treated with a single non-epinephrine modality and no epinephrine: **no error** — ACG accepts thermal/clip therapy alone; ESGE's combination preference for actively bleeding lesions is carried in the pearls, not scored against a concordant guideline.

**Management** keys on the rebleeding pathway: for a computed rebleeding or the missed lesion's haemorrhage, the plan point is dp4 = **repeat-endoscopy** — the move both guidelines put first (cap-mounted clip considered, T49; repeat endoscopy preferred over surgery/TAE, T50). TAE-first, surgery-first and supportive-only take later steps of the sequence first and earn no plan point. Control is the bleeds condition where a bleed runs; recognition and escalation follow the house rules.

## 5. Scenario configurations

- **Ongoing hematemesis** (`severity: "ongoing-hematemesis"` — the Milestone 28 mechanism): the airway-protection microstep m1.3 becomes required for the attempt. Sedation (m1.2) performed before it computes **err.unprotected-airway** (major) + **aspiration** onto airway.trachea at the sedation act (T52: aspiration among the sedation-related cardiopulmonary events, the largest share of this procedure's adverse events; T53). Protection performed late does not un-compute it. Recorded honestly in T53's register entry: neither governing guideline mandates prophylactic intubation for *all* upper GI bleeding, and the module does not claim they do — the rule is scoped to the authored ongoing-hematemesis scenario, where the sedation standard's own high-risk provisions apply.
- **The Dieulafoy variant** (§6).

## 6. Variant accounting

**Authored: Dieulafoy lesion** (`variant.dieulafoy-lesion`, source T54). Prevalence 1–6.5% of acute nonvariceal upper GI bleeding (5.8% in the consulted review's own series) — above the ≥1% variant gate at every point of the range. It adds one required microstep (m3.3, the caliber-persistent artery search of the proximal stomach/lesser curvature) and one register structure. Enforcement on the hernia pattern: completing the survey (m6.2) in this configuration without m3.3 resolves as **err.missed-variant** (critical-fail) — the unaccounted artery declares itself as a haemorrhage through the physiology engine at the verify-flagged placeholder rate (new parameter `bleed_rate_dieulafoy_ml_s`, 5.0 mL/s, the named-artery placeholder convention; no measured rate has been consulted and none is presented as fact), and the attempt FAILS. The consulted series reached this diagnosis at first endoscopy in 85% of cases — the miss is the configuration's known failure.

**Named non-variant: Mallory-Weiss tear.** A distinct lesion class (mucosal laceration at the gastro-oesophageal junction — neither an ulcer under Forrest discipline nor a vascular malformation); surveyed for and excluded at m2.1, not authored as a scenario in v1. No prevalence is asserted for it: none was consulted for this class in-session. The omission is a recorded decision (register entry `lesion.mallory-weiss-tear`).

**Scope exclusion: oesophageal varices.** Variceal hemorrhage is a different guideline family (vasoactive therapy, band ligation, TIPS pathways); this procedure is authored for **nonvariceal** bleeding — the stated scope of its governing guideline (T49). Varices found at survey end this procedure's remit via the dp1 abort-and-refer bailout (register entry `lesion.oesophageal-varices`).

**Not a variant: gastric ulcer.** The same lesion class under the same Forrest discipline; the scenario field names the site and the gate is site-independent (stated in the procedure's authority basis).

## 10. Sources and the register (T49–T54)

All sources were consulted in-session; each register entry states what it carries and how it was consulted. StatPearls/NCBI is cited for **nothing** (reCAPTCHA-blocked, as at every milestone).

- **T49 — ESGE 2021** (Gralnek et al., *Endoscopy* 2021;53:300–332, doi 10.1055/a-1369-5274). Main recommendations and the Table 1 statement list. The governing guideline: resuscitation, Blatchford, restrictive transfusion, timing, Forrest, combination therapy, the monotherapy prohibition, clot handling, rescue modalities, the recurrent-bleeding sequence (cap-mounted clip → TAE → surgery), high-dose PPI, no routine second-look.
- **T50 — ACG 2021** (Laine et al., *Am J Gastroenterol* 2021;116:899–917). The second governing guideline: therapy for spurting/oozing/visible vessel (st.6), modalities (st.8–9), epinephrine never alone (st.10), high-dose PPI × 3 days, repeat endoscopy preferred for rebleeding. Divergences from T49 (pre-endoscopy PPI; irrigation-resistant clot) recorded, not resolved.
- **T51 — Forrest classification and natural history** (Forrest et al., *Lancet* 1974, via the guidelines; further-bleeding rates from Laine & Peterson, *NEJM* 1994, whose stigmata table was consulted as reproduced in the ASGE VideoGIE review). Carries the carried figures exactly as the consulted table groups them: Ia/Ib 55% (grouped in that table), IIa 43%, IIb 22%, IIc 10%, III 5%.
- **T52 — ASGE adverse events of EGD** (Pasha et al., *Gastrointest Endosc* 2022;96:389–401.e1). Diagnostic perforation ≤0.03%; cardiopulmonary/sedation events the largest share (up to 60% of reported events; CORI 1-in-170 event rate, 1-in-10,000 mortality over 140,000 procedures). Figures consulted via the guideline's published summary and the ASGE guideline page; the register entry says so.
- **T53 — ASGE sedation and anesthesia guideline** (2018 update). The monitoring set, capnography, rescue capability, anesthesia-assistance provisions behind crit3 and the airway rules; consulted via the ASGE guideline page and its published summaries, and the register entry says so.
- **T54 — Dieulafoy review** (Zazour et al., *BMJ Open Gastroenterol* 2024;11:e001299), consulted in full text. Prevalence 1–6.5% of acute NVUGIH; mechanical therapy most effective (~90% permanent hemostasis); epinephrine suboptimal (early recurrence in two of three injection/APC-only cases in the series); first endoscopy diagnostic in 85%.
