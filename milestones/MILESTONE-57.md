Case #8 (`case.post-thyroid-hypocalcemia.008`) is the patient of Theatre
trace 30 — the total thyroidectomy of Milestone 56 — on postoperative days
1–2, presenting with the complication that operation's scoring *computed*:
symptomatic hypocalcemia from parathyroid failure, after the right

criterion priced a complication, and the complication is the case. The gate
working as designed and the patient symptomatic anyway are the same fact
read from two modules.

## Design rationale

**The handoff (M22 pattern, exactly).** The case's `theatre_link` records
the trace-30 debrief's SHA-256 and a `matched_fields` block copied from the
shipped file read in-session; `profile_case8` re-reads the file, recomputes
the hash, and matches every field mechanically (table below). The stage-1
finding `f.thyroidectomy-history` carries the same facts in prose, with the
hash in its text. The disposition *executes* the operation's dp6 decision
(calcium-replacement-protocol) — the loop is closed by management, not only
by citation. The Theatre tree is consumed read-only; `theatre_check` is
unmoved (736 / 0).

**The mimic.** The anchor hypothesis is anxiety with hyperventilation —
chosen because it is *honest*: hyperventilation alkalosis genuinely lowers
ionized calcium and produces perioral numbness, distal tingling and tetany
(C61), and the patient visibly breathes fast when frightened. Its
discriminators are therefore chemical, and each is a finding: the **total**
calcium is low (hyperventilation cannot lower it, C61), the **PTH** is low
(intact glands would raise it), and the venous **pH** is normal (no
alkalosis to drive the binding shift). The case never asks "is she
anxious?" — she is, and it is irrelevant.

**One LR, at the conservative end, with its counterweight attached.**
Trousseau's sign is the only finding any consulted source quantifies for
this differential: sensitivity 0.94 (C53, C55), false-positive rate 1%
(C55) to 1–4% (C53). The registered LR+ is **23.5** = 0.94 / 0.04, the
least favourable end — not 94.0. The derivation prose, the finding, and
this dossier all carry the counterweight: a 2016 post-thyroidectomy cohort
found the sign in only 23.5% of hypocalcemic patients (Chvostek 11.8%),
so in this population the sign performs far below the classic figures and
the board's stage-2 confidence (truth 0.9445; all five rivals retired)
outruns what the sign alone earns. The adjudication therefore rests on the
biochemistry and the tier block; Chvostek's sign carries no LR at all.

**The tier block is an assembly, disclosed as one.** No consulted source
publishes a three-tier management block, so the case builds
`hypocalcemia_management_tier` from thresholds three consulted sources
*do* state — ATA supplementation thresholds and calcitriol criterion
(C52), Fong & Khan's intravenous criteria (C53), Cooper & Gittoes' urgency
threshold (C54) — and says so in the block's `system` field. The result
travels in the adjudication as `severity_tier` (deliberately neither
`classification` nor `severity_grade`, both of which are spoken for by
other machinery in this module).

**A property discovered, not smoothed.** As first built, the dossier
draft claimed the tier block was "pending at stages 1–2". The first runner
execution refuted it: C53's symptomatic limb is unconditional, so the
block classifies `iv-calcium-indicated` from stage 1 on symptoms alone
(rule index 4), with the laboratory inputs still unknown, and
reclassifies under its first rule (index 0, symptomatic + corrected
< 1.9 mmol/L, all six inputs true) when the panel lands at stage 3. The
case prose, the adjudication basis, the criteria comparison and the
profile were all corrected to the computed behaviour — which is also the
clinically sharper fact: the ward *could* have acted on the symptoms at
stage 1, and the frame is what stopped it.

**The engine gap, in-case (M48 pattern).** The physiology engine has no
calcium axis — trace 30's own complication text says so verbatim. The
case's `physiology_link` records the pinned resting circulation by
data-identity with case 1 (no engine re-run asserted by the profile; the
hash was additionally re-verified by direct execution at this milestone —
`cargo run --release -- run resting` printed `trace hash:
0x6369b10e9a71c872` with all seven recorded outputs reproduced). The
calcium course (7.8 → 7.3 mg/dL) is authored data, every value checked
against C52–C54, with the albumin corrections re-derived by the checker.

## Anchoring analysis — the fifth shape

Exactly one event, recomputed by the checker: **(stage 2,
hyp.anxiety-hyperventilation)**, learner holding 0.26 against the
workbench's 0.0153. The series so far: clinician-frame (#4),
self-installed exposure (#5), result-installed (#6), social pressure
(#7). Case 8's is **premature closure by inheritance**:

- The frame was installed *by the record* before the case began — the
  ward note's attribution — and the learner adopted it wholesale: her
  stage-1 board leads with it (0.34) over the truth (0.30), the
  workbench (which weights the debrief as evidence) already leading
  with the truth at 0.42.
- It absorbed pre-existing discriminating evidence: the POD1 corrected
  calcium of 7.8 mg/dL — below the ATA line — was drawn, filed, and
  never acted on. Unlike case 6's BNP or case 7's pressured CTPA,
  nothing new had to arrive to break this frame; the failure was one of
  reading, not of information.
- It survived evidence the learner elicited herself: her own Trousseau
  examination at stage 2 moved her truth only to 0.52 while she held
  the frame at 0.26.
- It shaped her orders: at stage 1 she ordered only the bedside battery
  — not the calcium panel, available from stage 1 and already pointed
  at by the operation's dp6 protocol. The panel, ordered at stage 2, is
  what released her (frame 0.06 at stage 3; commit at 0.86, final
  Brier 0.0244 — the case's cost was a stage, not the diagnosis).
- The mimic's honesty is the lesson: the discriminating question is
  never "is she anxious?" but the total calcium, the PTH and the pH —
  the three legs the mimic cannot produce.

A second scored subtlety: the ECG classifies **non-contributory** (no LR,
no tier input) although its QTc (486 ms) is the reason the IV calcium runs
under cardiac monitoring. The case keeps the order and the verdict side by
side and records the verdict as a limit of the classification, in the
instrument's own output.

## Honest limits

- **Expert-review gate UNMET**, as for every case: no named reviewers
  exist, none are recorded, content is `dual-sourced-draft`, scores
  formative-only, no composite score anywhere.
- The Trousseau LR is the board's arithmetic from the consulted classic
  pair; its in-context sensitivity is materially lower (2016 cohort,
  carried in the derivation). The workbench's stage-2 retirement of all
  five rivals is a consequence of that single LR and is owned, here and
  in the case, rather than presented as five independent exclusions.
- The tier block is an assembly of three sources' thresholds, disclosed
  in its `system` field; it is not a published instrument.
- The AAES postoperative-hypocalcemia guidance was not separately
  registered for this case (it was not consulted in-session for the case
  register); the ATA statement (C52) carries the specialty-guidance role,
  and the AAES remains registered where it belongs — the Theatre's T44.
- Priors are verify-flagged authoring choices, not measurements; the
  truth's prior (0.42) deliberately leans on the loop evidence and says
  so in its note.
- Transient versus permanent hypoparathyroidism is unknowable at POD2
  (the 6-month line, C52); the case records the diagnosis at the time it
  is made and carries the question to follow-up.
- The 2016 cohort behind the Trousseau counterweight is cited in prose
  (finding, derivation, dossier) but not registered as a numbered source:
  its full citation was not pinned in-session, and the register does not
  carry what was not pinned.
