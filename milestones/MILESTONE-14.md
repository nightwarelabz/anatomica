# Milestone 14 — Reproductive system module v1

The reproductive module is built: a census of every
reproductive concept BodyParts3D 4.0 carries — male and female
lines censused separately — geometry resolution for all
thirteen records, a sixteen-record sourced clinical tranche,
and an independent checker passing 58/58. The module's
headline is an asymmetry the census makes exact: **the release
carries fourteen male reproductive concepts and exactly one
female one.** The entire internal female tract — ovary,
uterine tube, uterus, vagina, vulva — has no concept in the
release at all, and TotalSegmentator v1 has no reproductive
label either (0 of 104). The female line exists in this module
as fully sourced clinical anatomy recorded over a total
dataset absence, with the absence stated on the records
themselves.

Files:

- `data/reproductive/reproductive-inventory.json` — census
  (generated by `tools/repro_map.py`)
- `data/reproductive/reproductive-geometry.json` — per-record
  meshes (same generator)
- `data/reproductive/reproductive.json` — clinical tranche
  (generated by `tools/build_repro_records.py`)
- `tools/repro_check.py` — independent checker, shares no
  code with the generators

## Census — 21 concepts, 13 records

The universe is derived from eight IS-A lines/singletons and
three PART-OF lines in the release tables:

| set | concepts |
|---|---|
| testis IS-A line (class + R/L instances) | 3 |
| epididymis IS-A line | 3 |
| seminal vesicle IS-A line | 3 |
| prostate (both tables) | 1 |
| corpus cavernosum of penis (IS-A leaf) | 1 |
| corpus spongiosum of penis (IS-A leaf) | 1 |
| glans-zone IS-A line (zone + glans) | 2 |
| perineal muscle IS-A line (muscular cross-ref) | 3 |
| genital system PART-OF line (root + prostate) | 2 |
| perineum PART-OF line (perineum + anal part + sphincter) | 3 |
| mons pubis (PART-OF only) | 1 |
| **universe** | **21** (IS-A 17, PART-OF 6, both tables 2) |

By sex line: **male 14, female 1, shared 6** (the genital
root and the perineal concepts). The release's own genital
tree is vestigial — the PART-OF subtree under FMA7160
"genital system" has exactly two members, the root and the
prostate. Everything else male hangs in IS-A under generic
organ classes; the one female concept, the mons pubis
(FMA20218), is filed in PART-OF under the pelvis, there being
no female genital tree to file it under.

Records (13): six lateralised instances (right/left testis,
epididymis, seminal vesicle — class concept carried), the
prostate, the two corpora, the glans penis (zone concept
carried as its class), the mons pubis, the perineum and its
anal part. Representations: 10 whole-concept, 2 organ,
1 partof-concept. Dispositions partition the universe
exactly: 13 record, 4 class-carried (FMA7210, FMA18255,
FMA19386, FMA20247), 1 grouping-class (the genital root),
3 cross-reference:muscular (the perineal muscle line — the
external anal sphincter is the muscular module's record and
is never duplicated here), 0 subtree-internal.

Boundary closures, both gated by the checker:

- **Milestone 13 → 14:** the endocrine module's reproductive
  cross-reference set {FMA7210, FMA7211, FMA7212} is exactly
  this module's testis census.
- **Milestone 12 ↔ 14:** the urethra (FMA19667) is NOT in
  this universe. It is the urinary module's single unsexed
  record; the male pathway's clinical record states the
  relationship — the shared terminal channel of both systems —
  and the checker resolves the pointer to the urinary
  inventory record `urethra` and clinical record
  `structure.urethra`.

The cross-check sweep (36 reproductive terms over both parts
lists) rejected 13 names, every one adjudicated: five fornix
names are cerebral (nervous module — there is no vaginal
fornix concept), seven penile vessel names belong to the
cardiovascular module (its dorsal artery/vein records), and
the urethra to the urinary module.

## Geometry — 13/13 records, 20 mesh entries

Coverage is exact and complete for what the release carries:
all 13 records resolve meshes — male 10/10, female 1/1,
shared 2/2. Totals by streamed count: IS-A 3,284 vertices /
5,130 faces; PART-OF 7,726 vertices / 6,338 faces; combined
11,010 v / 11,468 f over 20 entries. Union bounding box
(source millimetres): min (−61.809, −200.861, 689.753),
max (59.957, −16.909, 828.900) — an extent of
121.8 × 184.0 × 139.1 mm. Two structural identities, both
re-derived and gated by the checker:

- **Glans/zone shared file.** The glans penis and its zone
  concept (FMA20247) resolve to the same single mesh file,
  FJ3134, in the IS-A table (neither concept is carried in
  PART-OF); the class therefore carries no geometry beyond
  the record's.
- **The perineum is drawn as its sphincter.** The perineum
  and its anal part carry the same four PART-OF files —
  {FJ1450, FJ1450M, FJ2543, FJ2548}, 990 v / 1,468 f — and
  those four files are exactly the file set of the muscular
  module's external-anal-sphincter record in both of its
  tables. FJ attribution across the module is one owner per
  file per table, with this sharing as the single exception.

The female line's only geometry, the mons pubis
(5,523 v / 2,960 f, PART-OF), is also the module's largest
single mesh. TotalSegmentator overlap is **exactly zero**:
no label among the 104 names any reproductive structure.

## Clinical tranche — 16 records, tiers A7 / B8 / C1

Sixteen records under the field-sourcing rule (values only
from the ten sources consulted in-session — TeachMeAnatomy
testes/epididymis, prostate, ovaries, uterus and perineum
articles; the NCI SEER duct-system module; OpenStax A&P 2e
§27.1 and §27.2; InnerBody; and the project's own datasets for
cross-module pointers — cited per field; unsourced fields are
null with a verify entry naming them). Tiers by group and by
sex line:

| group | A | B | C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Male pathway | 1 | | |
| Male organs and glands | 2 | 5 | |
| Female pathway | 1 | | |
| Female organs | 2 | 3 | 1 |
| Shared | 1 | | |
| **total** | **7** | **8** | **1** |

By sex line: male 8 records (A3/B5), female 7 (A3/B3/C1),
shared 1 (A1). Seven records link geometry; nine link none —
eight with the dataset absence recorded, and the male pathway
with its own no-geometry statement. Content gates in the
checker include both pathways' station orders (male:
seminiferous tubules → straight tubules → rete testis →
efferent ductules → epididymis → ductus deferens →
ejaculatory duct → prostatic → membranous → spongy urethra;
female: ovary → uterine tube → uterus → cervix → vagina),
the male pathway's gland order (seminal vesicles → prostate →
bulbourethral), the prostate's McNeal zones in order, and
every named cardiovascular pointer resolving (the testis
record's two testicular arteries and two testicular veins;
the penis record's two dorsal arteries and three dorsal
veins).

Three records carry the module's argument:

- **organ.ovary (tier A)** — fully sourced clinical anatomy
  (parts, ligaments, supply, drainage, innervation all cited)
  for a gonad the dataset does not contain; the absence is
  recorded on the record itself. Its arterial supply,
  unlike the testis', has no cardiovascular pointer to
  resolve: the release has no ovarian vessel concept either
  (checker-verified against the cardiovascular inventory).
- **organ.testis (tier A)** — the mirror image: geometry,
  vessels in the sister module, complete sourced record. The
  pair states the dataset's sex asymmetry in two records.
- **structure.mons-pubis (tier C)** — the female line's only
  geometry record is the tranche's thinnest: the consulted
  sources describe what the mons pubis is in a sentence and
  no more, so its parts, function and relations are flagged
  null. Recorded thin, not padded.

Flagged nulls elsewhere (each with its verify entry):
epididymis and seminal-vesicle arterial supply, prostate
lymphatic drainage, bulbourethral position, penile
innervation, uterine-tube regional subdivisions, vaginal
arterial supply and innervation, vulval arterial supply.

## Gap analysis — probe-verified, per sex line

Substring probes counted over all six release tables;
exact-name probes tested against both parts lists. Present
controls are interleaved in each line and all read present,
so the zeros are the tables', not the instrument's.

| probe family | male | female | shared |
|---|---|---|---|
| substring probes run | 21 | 26 | 7 |
| zero-hit (absent) | **17** | **25** | **5** |
| present controls | 4 | 1 | 2 |
| exact-name probes run | 7 | 22 | 4 |
| zero-hit (absent) | **7/7** | **22/22** | **4/4** |

**Male line — the duct system is missing.** `ductus
deferens`, `vas deferens`, `spermatic`, `ejaculat` and
`ampulla of ductus deferens` are all zero-hit: the pathway
is continuous in the data from testis to epididymis and
broken from there to the prostate — the segment a vasectomy
interrupts is the segment the data lacks. Also absent: the
scrotum and the testis coverings/interior (`scrotum`,
`tunica`, `dartos`, `cremaster`, `seminiferous`,
`rete testis`), the bulbourethral glands, the prepuce, and
the penis as an organ concept (exact-name zero; the organ
exists only as its erectile parts and its cardiovascular
vessels).

**Female line — everything internal is missing.** All 25
non-control substring probes are zero-hit: ovary and ovarian
structures, the whole tract (uterus, uterine, fallopian,
fimbria, cervix, vagina), the uterine wall layers
(endometrium, myometrium, perimetrium), the ligaments
(broad ligament, mesovarium, mesosalpinx, mesometrium), and
the external genitalia except the mons pubis (vulva,
clitoris, labia, hymen, Bartholin's and Skene's glands).
All 22 exact-name probes — ovary, uterus, vagina, clitoris,
uterine tube, cervix, vulva, the labia, the ligaments, the
follicle, the corpus luteum — name no concept. This extends
the absence Milestone 13 recorded for the ovary at the
endocrine boundary to the entire tract, and compounds it:
the cardiovascular module has no ovarian vessels, the
nervous census no pudendal supply, and TotalSegmentator no
labels — no adopted dataset in the project carries the
female reproductive system at all. Completing it needs a
new source (the full-FMA/TA class of fix already named for
the nervous module's nerve concepts), not more mapping of
this release.

**Shared line — the perineum's contents are missing.**
`perineal body`, `perineal membrane`, `ischioanal`,
`perineal pouch` and `sphincter urethrae` are zero-hit; the
region's clinical contents exist as sourced text on the
perineum record, and its geometry is one sphincter.

## Checker

`tools/repro_check.py` — independent of both generators —
re-derives the universe, records, dispositions, sweep,
probes, boundary closures and TS overlap from the six
tables, re-measures all 20 geometry entries (every vertex/
face count and bounding box), re-derives both structural
identities, and re-checks the tranche's citation/flag/tier
discipline plus the content gates above: **58/58 checks
passed.**
