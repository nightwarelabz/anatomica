# Milestone 15 — Integumentary system module v1

The integumentary module is built: a census of every
integumentary concept BodyParts3D 4.0 carries, geometry
resolution for all six records, a thirteen-record sourced
clinical tranche, and an independent checker passing 39/39.
It is the last of the eleven system modules, and it is also
the emptiest dataset in the project: **the release's whole
integument is twelve concepts and four mesh files** — one
undifferentiated whole-body skin shell and a handful of
hair concepts. The skin's layers, the nails, the cutaneous
glands and the mammary gland have no concept in the release
at all, and the mammary gland posed the module's one true
classification question: it is filed by the release under
*neither* candidate system, so its ownership is decided
here by adjudication, on a stated basis, with the
counter-consideration recorded on the record itself.

Files:

- `data/integumentary/integumentary-inventory.json` — census
  (generated by `tools/integ_map.py`)
- `data/integumentary/integumentary-geometry.json` —
  per-record meshes (same generator)
- `data/integumentary/integumentary.json` — clinical
  tranche (generated by `tools/build_integ_records.py`)
- `tools/integ_check.py` — independent checker, shares no
  code with the generators

## Census — 12 concepts, 6 records

The universe is derived from three IS-A lines and two
PART-OF lines in the release tables:

| set | concepts |
|---|---|
| skin IS-A (a leaf under "nonparenchymatous organ") | 1 |
| subdivision-of-epidermis IS-A line | 6 |
| hair-set IS-A line (under "set of organ regions") | 3 |
| integumentary-system PART-OF line (a bare root) | 1 |
| integument PART-OF line | 2 |
| **universe** | **12** (IS-A 10, PART-OF 3, both tables 1) |

By line: skin 1, appendage 9, system 2. Records (6): the
skin (an organ record keyed on its both-table concept), the
hair class (its parent class, skin appendage, carried), the
three regional hairs — hair of head, hair of trunk, pubic
hair — and the eyebrow (its set class, set of facial hairs,
carried). Dispositions partition the universe exactly: 6
record, 2 class-carried (FMA71012, FMA70741), 4
grouping-class (the system root FMA72979, the integument
FMA74657, the subdivision of epidermis FMA70593, the set of
hairs FMA70752), 0 subtree-internal.

How the release represents the system, in its own filings:

- **The named system root is empty.** FMA72979
  "integumentary system" hangs directly under "human body"
  in PART-OF and has no children at all. The only PART-OF
  composition in the system is integument → skin.
- **The skin has no parts.** In IS-A the skin is a leaf
  under "nonparenchymatous organ"; in PART-OF its only
  parent is the integument. No regional skin, and no layer:
  there is no concept named "epidermis" and none named
  "dermis" in either parts list (exact-name probe-verified;
  "dermis" is probed by exact name only, since as a
  substring it hides inside "epidermis"). The one
  layer-named concept, FMA70593 "subdivision of
  epidermis", is a tissue-subdivision class — and the
  release files the entire appendage line under it: skin
  appendage → hair → hair of head / hair of trunk → pubic
  hair.
- **A second hair line** hangs under "set of organ
  regions": set of hairs → set of facial hairs → eyebrow,
  the only named facial hair in the release. There is no
  eyelash concept.
- **Pubic hair crosses a module boundary in its filing:**
  IS-A under hair of trunk (this module), PART-OF under the
  mons pubis (FMA20218) — the reproductive module's only
  female record. The concept is owned here; the boundary
  closes by construction and is gated by the checker
  against the reproductive inventory.

The cross-check sweep (20 terms over both parts lists)
rejected 59 names, every one adjudicated: 19 are the
ciliary/nasociliary family — ocular structures and their
autonomic supply, censused by the nervous module (none is
an eyelash); 16 are glands that are not cutaneous — the
adrenal and pituitary (endocrine module), the salivary
line (digestive module), the lacrimal gland (the eye's
apparatus, censused by no module) and one generic component
class; 24 are the release's deep fascial system and the
tensor fasciae latae muscle (muscular module) — deep
investing fascia of the limb compartments, filed under a
fascial root of its own, not skin and not the subcutaneous
layer. Twelve sweep terms hit nothing at all, and they are
the module's headline in miniature: *nail, unguis,
mammary, breast, sebaceous, sweat, follicle, eyelash,
scalp, subcutaneous, areola, nipple*.

## Geometry — 6/6 records, 9 mesh entries, 4 files

Coverage is exact and complete for what the release
carries: all six records resolve meshes. The whole
module's geometry, across both tables and all twelve
concepts, is **four mesh files**:

| file | carried by | vertices | faces |
|---|---|---|---|
| FJ2810 | skin (both tables); integument and system root (PART-OF) | 102,467 | 203,382 |
| FJ2813 | hair of head; the hair, appendage and subdivision classes | 12,464 | 18,006 |
| FJ2815 | hair of trunk, pubic hair (both tables); the same classes | 5,523 | 2,960 |
| FJ2812 | eyebrow; set of facial hairs; set of hairs | 17,143 | 7,912 |

Summed per record (entries, sharing counted): IS-A 161,107
v / 256,186 f over 7 entries; PART-OF 107,990 v / 206,342 f
over 2 entries. Union bounding box (source millimetres):
min (−334.119, −246.783, −78.111), max (332.825, 45.248,
1651.630) — an extent of 666.9 × 292.0 × 1729.7 mm, the
whole-body shell dominating as expected. Three structural
identities, all re-derived and gated by the checker:

- **One shell serves three filings.** Skin in IS-A, skin
  in PART-OF, the integument and the integumentary system
  root all resolve to FJ2810 alone: system, organ division
  and organ are the same mesh.
- **The hair files partition head from trunk.** The
  subdivision, appendage and hair classes each carry
  {FJ2813, FJ2815}; hair of head carries FJ2813 alone;
  hair of trunk and pubic hair carry FJ2815 alone (pubic
  hair in both tables). FJ attribution is therefore shared
  by design, and the checker gates the exact sharing map.
- **The eyebrow's set classes carry nothing beyond it.**
  Set of hairs, set of facial hairs and eyebrow all
  resolve to FJ2812 in IS-A; none is carried in PART-OF.

TotalSegmentator overlap is **zero skin labels**, with one
adjudication: of the 104 TS v1 labels, exactly one matches
an integumentary term — `face`, present in all 1,204
subjects. It is a mask of the facial region, an imaging
region: it names no skin, layer or appendage. The v1.0
label set contains no `skin`, `body_trunc`,
`body_extremities` or any other body-surface label — the
package class map, the Milestone 3 index census and
labels.json agree on the same 104 labels — so there is no
TS skin segmentation to adopt, and the hypothesised
body-region masks do not exist in this dataset version.

## The mammary adjudication

The mammary gland is the one structure in the census whose
*ownership* — not merely its presence — had to be decided,
and the dataset refuses to decide it: "mammary", "breast",
"mamma", "nipple", "areola" and "lactiferous" score zero
hits in both parts lists and all six release tables. The
release files the gland under neither the integumentary
nor the reproductive system; there is nothing in the data
for either module to own, and the reproductive module
(Milestone 14) completed its census claiming no mammary
name — the checker re-verifies that it holds no mammary or
breast record or concept.

The decision, recorded on the clinical record itself
(`organ.mammary-gland`, field `ownership`): **this module
owns it**, on the basis that the consulted clinical source
(TeachMeAnatomy, The Breasts) defines the mammary glands
as **modified sweat glands** — cutaneous glands by kind,
of a piece with this module's sebaceous and sweat gland
records, and developmentally an appendage of the skin. The
counter-consideration is recorded alongside, not
suppressed: the same source introduces the glands as "an
accessory gland of the female reproductive system", and
lactation is reproductive function. The placement is a
stated convention of this project, not a fact of the
dataset; the reproductive module cross-references the
record by id rather than duplicating it.

## Clinical tranche — 13 records, tiers A11 / C2

Thirteen records under the field-sourcing rule (values
only from the five sources consulted in-session — OpenStax
A&P 2e §5.1, §5.2 and §5.3; TeachMeAnatomy, The Breasts;
and the project's own datasets for dataset facts and
cross-module pointers — cited per field; unsourced fields
are null with a verify entry naming them). Tiers by group:

| group | A | B | C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Skin and its layers | 4 | | |
| Appendages | 6 | | 2 |
| The mammary gland | 1 | | |
| **total** | **11** | | **2** |

Six records link geometry; seven link none, each with the
dataset absence recorded on the record. Content gates in
the checker include the skin's layer order (epidermis →
dermis → hypodermis), the epidermis' five strata in
deep-to-superficial order, the dermis' two layers, the
hair and nail part orders, the sweat glands' types in
eccrine-then-apocrine order, and the mammary record's
15–20 lobules and its lymphatic percentages in order
(axillary 75% → parasternal 20% → posterior intercostal
5%).

Three records carry the module's argument:

- **structure.epidermis (tier A)** — fully sourced anatomy
  of a layer the dataset subdivides but never names: five
  strata in order, its cells, its thin/thick forms. Its
  record states the dataset position plainly — the
  release's "subdivision of epidermis" is a class carrying
  the appendages' geometry, not an epidermis.
- **organ.mammary-gland (tier A)** — complete sourced
  anatomy (parts, relations, supply, drainage with its
  percentages, innervation, the prolactin/oxytocin note)
  for a gland the dataset does not contain, with the
  absence and the ownership adjudication on the record.
- **structure.hair-of-trunk and structure.pubic-hair
  (tier C, the tranche's two)** — the dataset gives each
  region a record and a mesh; the consulted literature
  gives neither a separate account, so each record's
  function field is flagged null rather than borrowing the
  general hair functions as if they were region-specific.
  Recorded thin, not padded (the mons pubis precedent,
  Milestone 14).

Flagged nulls elsewhere: none — every other field in the
tranche is valued and cited; TA names are null and flagged
on all thirteen records (the TA2 pass remains owed).

## Gap analysis — probe-verified

Substring probes over all six release tables: **35 of 41
zero-hit** — the six present are the controls (skin, hair,
eyebrow, integument, epidermis-via-subdivision, pubic
hair). Exact-name probes: **29 of 34 zero-hit** — the five
present are the controls. The absent set is the system's
entire substance below the shell:

- **The layers:** epidermis, dermis and hypodermis as
  concepts; every stratum; keratinocyte, melanocyte,
  Langerhans and Merkel as names; the dermal papilla.
- **The follicle and glands:** hair follicle, sebaceous,
  sweat/sudoriferous, eccrine, apocrine, arrector — the
  release carries no cutaneous gland of any kind.
- **The nail:** in every form and every part — nail,
  unguis, lunule, eponychium, cuticle; fingernail, toenail,
  nail bed and nail plate by exact name.
- **The mammary gland:** under every name (above).
- **The specialisations:** scalp, eyelash, beard, vellus,
  lanugo; the subcutaneous layer under both its names
  (subcutaneous tissue, superficial fascia by exact name);
  the sensory corpuscles (pacinian, meissner) and corium.

As with the nerve concepts (M9), the gland interiors (M13)
and the female tract (M14), completing this system needs a
new source — the full-FMA/TA class of fix — not more
mapping of this release. What this release does carry, it
carries exactly: a skin shell measured at 102,467 vertices
and a hair partition in two files.

## Checker

`tools/integ_check.py` — independent of both generators —
re-derives the universe, records, dispositions, sweep,
probes, boundary closure and TS adjudication from the six
tables, re-measures all 9 geometry entries (every
vertex/face count and bounding box), re-derives the three
structural identities and the four-file census, and
re-checks the tranche's citation/flag/tier discipline plus
the content gates above, including the mammary ownership
gate and the reproductive cross-module gate: **39/39
checks passed.**

adopted geometry is complete for what the release carries

tract, gland interiors and ducts (lymphatic, endocrine,
urinary), the pleura and respiratory zone, the sigmoid and
biliary terminus, and the integument's layers, nails and
glands. None of these is a mapping problem; each needs a
source decision (the full-FMA/TA class of fix already named
at Milestone 9), and none has been filled from anything
less.
