## 1. The mechanisms as modelled

**Venomotor recruitment (baroreflex effector 3).** A new state
`veno_ml` shifts the systemic venous unstressed volume:
`P_sv = (V_sv − (Vu_sv − veno_ml)) / C_sv` — recruited volume *is*
stressed volume, which is the mechanism (S13). The reflex computes
a target `capacity × clamp(gain × err, −1, 1)` from the same
normalised MAP error signal the HR and resistance effectors use,
and the state approaches it first-order at the cited time constant:
`d(veno)/dt = (target − veno) / τ_v`, τ_v = 60 s. The swing is
symmetric (± capacity), as in the source model.

**Transcapillary refill.** Two new states: `cap_lp`, the low-passed
mean systemic capillary pressure (same 3 s sensing lag as the MAP
channel), and `refill_ml`, the net cumulative interstitial→plasma
transfer. When the reflex engages, the current `cap_lp` is captured
as the reference `Pcap_ref` — the model's own resting capillary
pressure, the same self-referencing device as the MAP setpoint.
Flow is the lumped Starling form
`Q_refill = k × (Pcap_ref − cap_lp)`, integrated into the systemic
venous compartment and bounded by the reserve state
`|refill_ml| ≤ refill_reserve_ml` in both directions (refill after
loss; escape after infusion). The interstitium is not a
compartment; `refill_ml` is an external inflow for accounting,
exactly like the event channel's integral, and
`total_volume_ml()` subtracts both — the conservation identity
still closes (≤ 2×10⁻⁹ mL in every run below).

Both mechanisms are integrated by the same fixed-order RK4 at the
same substep, and both states (plus `cap_lp`) are folded into the
per-tick FNV trace hash. Determinism re-verified: two resting runs
bit-identical; snapshot/restore replay identical (tests).

New sources consulted this session: **S13** — Scarsoglio &
Ridolfi's Frontiers supplementary model description (the
Ursino-lineage baroreflex parameter set: venous effector equations
S27–S28, Tables S1/S7 read in full). **S14** — Lanne & Lundvall,
Acta Physiol Scand 1992 (human LBNP refill rates 0.053/0.088/0.147

"Fluid Shifts After Hemorrhage", a *secondary* educational review
(its secondary status is recorded in the source entry): human
volunteer studies — ~25% of a moderate loss compensated by fluid
recruitment within 30 minutes; 25–50% within the first hour; refill
restores 500–1000 mL in 1–2 hours. **S16** — Rossaint et al.,
Critical Care 2016 (4th ed.), Table 2, the ACS/ATLS classification
re-read in full this session: Class II (750–1500 mL) pulse
100–120/min, systolic pressure Normal, pulse pressure Decreased;
Class III (1500–2000 mL) pulse 120–140/min, pressures Decreased;
Class IV pulse >140/min. The Mutschler caveat stands on this table
exactly as Milestone 8 recorded it (S7): ATLS magnitudes are
contested; the gates below are ATLS-*band* checks, reported as
such, not claims that the bands are precise physiology.

## 3. Calibration disclosure

Two calibrations were performed, both inside the rules above, both
reported with their sweeps (sweep harness and full outputs retained
in the build log):

**(a) Reflex gains.** With the sourced mechanisms active, the two
placeholder gains were swept on a grid (venomotor gain × HR gain ∈
{6,12,20,30} × {4,8,12}), scoring the resting case, both
haemorrhages, and the refill observation. The measured frontier is
a trade-off: stronger venomotor gain restores pressure and
*lowers* the heart-rate response; the ATLS Class II heart-rate
band (≥100/min) is reachable only in the grid's lower-venomotor /
higher-HR-gain region, and no grid cell restores systolic pressure
to ≥90 mmHg (best cell 89.5). The shipped cell is **venomotor gain
12.0 (the initial value, unchanged), HR gain 12.0** — chosen
because it satisfies every *hard* gate simultaneously: resting MAP
and CO unmoved to two decimals, −750 heart rate 103.9 (band
100–120), refill observation in band, all directional lines
passing. The systolic line it cannot satisfy is carried as a
disclosed soft miss (§4), not tuned away and not hidden.

**(b) Refill conductance.** Swept k ∈ {0.03…0.05} in the
observation case: 30-minute refill 153 / 174 / **194** / 213 / 231
mL. Shipped **k = 0.04**, landing at 194 mL = 25.9% of the loss,
on S15's cited ~25% point. Identification is trajectory-level and
is disclosed as such in the parameter note: the model's driving
deficit is small (~3.6 mmHg at bleed end — the lumped capillary
segment's pressure is flow-dominated, ~63 mmHg at rest), so k is a
net whole-body lumped coefficient against that signal, not a
capillary filtration coefficient.

## 5. Theatre impact (Milestone 17 re-run)

The theatre crate consumes this engine by path dependency. Its
`cargo test --release` (8/8), `run-all` (5 traces, 0 expectation
failures), and `tools/theatre_check.py` (131 checks, 0 failures)
all pass under v1. One theatre code change was required and is
disclosed here: the runner's volume identity measured blood loss
as (start + infused − end) over raw compartment sums; refill
during an attempt is now a real inflow into those compartments,
so the identity adds back net refill (`engine.net_refill_ml()`),
exactly as it already added infused volume. Without it, the
identity would silently report loss-minus-refill as "blood loss".
With it, **blood-loss figures are unchanged everywhere**:

- Shipped traces (no bleeding): loss 0.0 mL in all five, before
  and after. A field-by-field diff of the regenerated debriefs
  against the Milestone 17 originals shows **exactly four changed
  fields per debrief**: `engine_trace_hash` (hash format, above);
  `map_nadir_mmhg` 72.9110748 → 72.9113712 (+0.0003 mmHg — all five
  traces bleed nothing, so the nadir is the resting point);
  `engine_beats` (+101…+252 — the calibrated HR gain raises the
  resting rate slightly, so more beats elapse in the same simulated
  span); `engine_conservation_error_ml` (~10⁻¹⁰ → ~10⁻⁹ scale,
  RK4 over more states). **No verdict, checklist, safety, process,
  physiology, or management score changed in any trace** —
  physiology remains 7/7 in all five.
- Synthetic probes: unclipped cystic division — loss 750.0 mL by
  the event integral and 750.0 mL by the engine identity (exact
  agreement, where the theatre's own test tolerance is 1.0 mL);
  **MAP nadir 45.6 → 68.1 mmHg; time below the rubric threshold
  248 s → 0 s**; physiology subscore 5/7 (nadir points now full at
  2/2; the 0/2 loss points are the rubric's volume band for a
  750 mL loss, an engine-independent figure). Replaced-RHA probe —
  loss 1,500.0 mL both routes, verdict FAIL unchanged, MAP nadir
  39.5 mmHg, 234 s below threshold, physiology 1/7 (a 30%-loss
  decompensation, consistent with §4). Second-branch probe — loss
  360.0 mL both routes, nadir 71.1 mmHg, physiology 6/7.

Milestone 17's caveat — "the MAP response magnitude inherits
Milestone 8's disclosed limitation… the *pressure magnitudes* are
not yet validated" — is **retired for the 750 mL class of loss**
by §4 (heart-rate magnitude now gated against S16; MAP magnitude
reported against a compensated picture) and **stands for the
1,500 mL class**, where the model decompensates (§4). The
blood-loss *volumes* were exact before and remain exact.

**Still NOT validated / known-missed, with mechanisms:**

  (soft miss, §4). The 90 bound itself was never read in a
  consulted source (Milestone 8's disclosure, inherited).
- −1,500 mL presentation: the model decompensates (MAP 29) where
  the Class III table describes a sustained, decreased pressure.
  Compensation ceiling = cited recruitment (630 mL) + refill,
  with **no contractility effector** — the binding omission.
- Infusion magnitudes: improved, still above resting ranges at
  the post window; no renal excretion or lymphatic return exists
  to clear a sustained load.
- The model's refill state is *gross* interstitial→plasma
  transfer; the cited volunteer figure is *net* plasma-volume
  compensation (which also nets out renal handling the model
  lacks). Over 30 minutes the difference is second-order; over
  hours it is not modelled at all.
- Refill fluid is treated as volume-identical to plasma: no
  oncotic dilution (real refill is protein-poor and
  self-limiting), no haematocrit change, no interstitial
  compliance/pressure state.
- All reflex *gains* remain verify-flagged placeholders (one now
  calibrated to a published band — calibration is not sourcing);
  venomotor gain and the HR/resistance bounds likewise.
